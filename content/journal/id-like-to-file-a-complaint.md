first day at the new job and it's the one. i knew about an hour in. don't ask me how, i just did.

the alarm went off at a time i'm not going to write down because you'll judge me. then a train, a different train, a bus, and then a walk that nobody mentioned anywhere, and i read everything they sent me, twice. three changes. i'm fine with it and i'm also going to bring it up every single day until someone moves the building.

sunny the whole way though, actual sun, kissing my skin, and yes i'm keeping that phrase, it's my website.

they gave me a macbook, brand new, with an apple keyboard and an apple mouse, because this company loves apple. the screens are apple, the chargers are apple, i think the plants are apple. i sat down like i'd been there for years, which is a lie but a good one. also there's a snack area with fridges full of soda and honestly that should have been in the job listing, above the pay.

anyway the interns take out the trash. all of us, at the end of the day, in a line, and i'm the youngest in the building so i'm also the cheapest, which is how the numbers work i'm told, and i didn't agree to the numbers.

so yes i have a job and it's a good one and it ate my afternoons on day one. that's the whole complaint. thank you for reading it.
